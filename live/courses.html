A career in passive fire protection

Build a career in a specialist trade.

Fire stopping and fire door installation are skilled, safety-critical trades. Clients increasingly ask installers to show their training.

  • A certificate, a diploma and a route to NVQ

    Get an Academy certificate after Theory and a diploma after Practical. Work towards NVQ Level 2 once you meet the assessment requirements.

  • Precise work, less heavy lifting

    The work is about measuring, fitting, sealing and checking. Physical demands vary by role and site.

  • Demand driven by building safety rules

    The law requires fire stopping and fire doors to be installed by competent people. In England, responsible persons in many multi-occupied residential buildings must check fire doors regularly.

  • Taught the way our own teams work

    Lessons are filmed on real Aleron projects, using the materials, details and records you’ll meet on site.

FAQs

Before you enrol.

Can I start with no fire protection experience?

Yes. Start with the online Theory course to learn the fundamentals. Talk to us before buying Practical or NVQ so we can check what experience and site access you’ll need.

How is the online course delivered?

Video and text lessons you can take on your phone or computer, at your own pace, followed by an online assessment.

Where and how does the Practical take place?

A full day of hands-on training in a small group with the Aleron team. You can book it once you’ve passed the Theory assessment. We’ll confirm dates and location when you book.

Can I move on from Theory to Practical or NVQ later?

Yes. Get in touch and we’ll upgrade you. Your total stays the same as buying the package in one go.

Who assesses the NVQ?

The NVQ Level 2 is assessed by an independent assessor using evidence from your own site work. You need to meet the assessment requirements, including suitable site work. Ask us before buying if you’re not sure.

Does the Academy certificate come with a CSCS card?

No. Aleron Academy issues its own certificate and diploma. CSCS issues cards separately and sets its own requirements. See Blue Skilled Worker card requirements.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. For three or more people we offer group prices and can invoice your company. Ask for a team quote.
