Why fire stopping

What we hear from people who made the move, and what we see as a contractor hiring for it.

  • Strong pay rates

    Experienced fire stoppers are advertised at day rates well above many general site roles.

  • Lighter on your body

    Precise, methodical work with sealants, boards and collars rather than heavy lifting all day.

  • Demand backed by building safety rules

    The law requires fire stopping to be installed by competent people, and clients increasingly ask for proof of training.

  • Taught by a working contractor

    Aleron Fire Protection installs passive fire protection on live projects. You learn the way our own teams work.
